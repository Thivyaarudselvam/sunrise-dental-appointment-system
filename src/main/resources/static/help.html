Help & User Guide

Use your staff username and password on the login screen. Default account: admin / admin123.

Before booking an appointment, make sure at least one Patient, Dentist and Treatment are registered using the respective menu items on the Dashboard.

Go to "Register / Search Appointment", select the patient, dentist and treatment from the dropdowns, choose a date and time, then click Register Appointment. Note down the appointment number shown.

On the same page, enter the appointment number in the Search box to view full appointment details.

Go to "Generate Bill", enter the appointment number and click Generate Bill. The system automatically calculates the total (consultation fee + treatment cost) and displays a printable receipt.

Click "Logout" at the top right of any page to safely end your session.
